How it works

1

Scope & identify

Break the process into activities and capture the material risks as cause → event → consequence, mapped to a small taxonomy.

2

Rate & control

Score inherent risk (L×I), assess each control's design and operating effectiveness separately, then re-rate residual on controls as they actually operate.

3

Appetite & action

Compare residual with a stated appetite; every breach gets a prioritised, owned action. The profile summary tallies it automatically.
